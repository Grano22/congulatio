A forge for role-playing game worlds: create characters and their 3D builds, write branching dialogue, map relations and routines, and simulate how souls with different motives play a scenario out.

Author
Adrian Blasiak
Purpose
University project
Built with
Angular, NgRx Signals, three.js, Lucide icons, Mixamo animations

Use of AI

Parts of this application were developed with the help of an AI assistant (Claude by Anthropic). The idea, requirements, design decisions and the review and acceptance of every change are the author’s own, and the author takes responsibility for the final work.

The Story AI panel in the app uses the language model built into Chrome. It runs on this device, and every dialogue step it drafts is marked “AI draft” in the Tree.
